# Brief: Prospett Marketing

## The business

Prospett Marketing is a marketing agency in Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil (WhatsApp DDD 51). It positions itself as "Estratégia humana. Execução com IA." and says it connects content, Google, artificial intelligence, automation and data to turn attention into business opportunities.

## Who it is for

Small and medium local businesses in Rio Grande do Sul whose owner or manager has no structured marketing team. Chosen by the founder for this analysis.

## What it sells and at what price

A monthly retainer of R$ 2,000 to R$ 4,000 (Brazilian reais) for strategy and execution. The free diagnostic is the entry point. The site lists six fronts:

1. Presença nas IAs (GEO/AEO): content oriented to real questions, technical SEO and structured data, consistency of information and mention analysis.
2. Google e aquisição: Google Ads, SEO, local presence, conversion pages and measurement.
3. Conteúdo e autoridade: editorial planning, social media, articles and scripts, with AI support and human review.
4. Automação de marketing e vendas: forms, CRM, WhatsApp and e-mail connected to qualification and follow-up.
5. Sites e landing pages: fast, responsive, conversion-oriented and machine-readable.
6. Inteligência e desempenho: dashboards, campaign tracking and analysis.

The site explicitly says organic AI presence has no guarantee of citations or recommendations.

## Where and how

Online operation, remote service, WhatsApp contact. Customers are expected to arrive through referrals, active prospecting, content and Google search. Delivery is monthly with follow-up and reports. The site's primary call to action is a free "diagnóstico" form.

## What already exists

- A pre-rendered static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, no runtime dependencies) in the repository.
- Brand and logo already exist.
- A published preview at `prospett-marketing.abeloas.chatgpt.site` (a ChatGPT Sites preview domain, not a custom domain).
- WhatsApp number (51) 99550-1677 is live.
- No real cases, clients or results are published; the site says examples are illustrative and real cases require data and authorization.

## Known gaps at the time of this analysis

- `dist/config.js` has `formEndpoint: ''`, so the diagnostic form cannot send leads and the site reports unavailability rather than simulating success.
- `email` and `privacyContact` are empty in the same file.
- No custom domain; canonical and Open Graph URLs point to the preview domain.
- No published pricing on the site.
- The team size, current monthly costs, existing client base, capacity to serve new clients and available marketing budget were not provided.

## Open questions for the board

- Is "presença nas IAs" a real, urgent pain for local SMBs in RS, or a feature looking for a market?
- Can an agency with no published cases and no proof charge R$ 2,000 to R$ 4,000 a month?
- Is the offer too broad (six fronts) for a small team to deliver well?
- How do the first 100 clients arrive, and at what acquisition cost?
- What stops a local competitor, or an AI tool, from copying this in six months?

## Constraints

- No invented budget, market size or competitor facts. Where a number is missing, name it as an open question and say which skill answers it (competitors, consumer panel, CFO).
